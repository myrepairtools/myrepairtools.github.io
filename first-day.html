Get ready for your first day

1Bring your ID

Federal law says we have to see your original documents in person before you start — copies and photos don’t count. Bring one of these:

US passport (or passport card)
On its own — nothing else needed
Permanent Resident Card (green card)
On its own — nothing else needed
Employment Authorization Document (EAD)
On its own — nothing else needed
Driver’s license or state ID + Social Security card
Two documents
Driver’s license or state ID + birth certificate
Two documents
Not sure, or yours isn’t on the list? Call your store before your first day — numbers are at the bottom — and we’ll sort it out.

2Install QuickBooks Workforce

This is how you clock in and out, so have it installed and signed in before you arrive. Use the email address you gave us on your new-hire form.

iPhone — App Store Android — Google Play
Look for an invite email from Intuit and finish the setup it walks you through. If it hasn’t arrived by the day before you start, call your store.

3What to wear

  • Shirt — the black CPR polo or 1/2-zip we provide. Yours has to be ordered, so it usually won’t be ready on day one. Wear a plain black shirt until it arrives — ask if we have a spare in your size.
  • Pants — blue or black jeans without rips or heavy distressing, or khakis.
  • Shoes — closed-toe, clean, in good shape. No sandals.
  • No hats on duty, CPR-branded or otherwise.

Keep jewelry minimal, facial hair trimmed, and cover any tattoo that a customer might find offensive.

4Parking

Park in the back and come in through the front. Leave the spots out front for customers.

Call the store you’re joining, or email bbay@cpr-stores.com.
