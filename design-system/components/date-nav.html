Components

Date Navigator

The house period control. One joined white box: back arrow, clickable label, forward arrow — no caret. The label opens a calendar popover; arrow-stepping alone is never enough.

The Navigator

One joined box, segments split by 1px borders. The “Today / This Week / This Month” jump pill renders only when you are looking at a non-current period — it is the way back, not decoration.

Current period — no jump pill

Past period — jump pill appears

Week variant, forward limit reached

Do not restyle locally. .cpr-navbox and .cpr-navjump are injected by assets/pickers.js. Load the script and use the classes.

Popovers

Opened by clicking the label. Week mode highlights the whole row; day mode selects one date; month mode pages by year. Out-of-range periods grey out rather than disappearing.
