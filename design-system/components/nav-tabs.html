Tabs, Pills & Chips

Three related families that are easy to confuse. Tabs switch views. Pills report status and never take a click. Chips are compact, clickable data.

Tabs

A joined white box with a blue active segment. The active tab persists to localStorage and mirrors into the URL hash so refresh and deep links both land right.

Required behavior. Valid hash > localStorage > default at load. Every switch does both lsSet and history.replaceState — never pushState, never a bare #.

Status Pills

Uppercase micro-type on a pale wash. Read-only — if it can be clicked it is a chip or a button, not a pill. (The one exception is the mobile time-off row, where the pill is the action sheet trigger.)
