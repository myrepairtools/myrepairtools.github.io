Photography

Photography shows the connection between technology, people, and their lives — never a product shot on white. Any photo carrying text gets the dark-blue filter first.

Content Direction

Do
  • Two or more people interacting with technology positively
  • Fresh, familiar-looking models — not stock-perfect
  • Users having fun, learning, or socializing
  • Natural light, real environments
Don’t
  • Isolated devices on white seamless
  • Cover a model’s face with text or the logo
  • Heavy color grades that fight the palette
  • Photos so busy the logo can’t clear the exclusion zone

The Dark-Blue Filter

Before any text goes on a photo, lay Blue Dark #2D2D3B over it at 40–60% transparency. This is the only approved way to make a photo readable — never a blur, never a black scrim.
