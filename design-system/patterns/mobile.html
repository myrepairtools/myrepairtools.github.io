Bottom tab barHome · Tasks · My Time · Commission · More. Fixed, safe-area padded, with a pinned view-transition-name. “More” opens the slide-in menu and replaces the hamburger.
Fixed footers--cpr-bb-h is 0 on desktop and the bar height on mobile. Any page with its own fixed footer must sit at bottom: var(--cpr-bb-h, 0px).
Safe areasStandalone installs get html.mrt-standalone and --cpr-top-h grows by env(safe-area-inset-top) so the iOS status bar never crowds the top bar.
Tap targets44px minimum on every mobile action. Phone numbers and emails are tel: / mailto: links, not plain text.
Square buttonHides on mobile; it lives as a row under More instead.

Breakpoints

≥ 1000px
Dense ledger layouts. Label→value rows instead of stacked cards.
< 860px
App mode: bottom tab bar replaces the hamburger; assistant chat becomes a full-screen sheet.
< 720px
Calendars compress; chips shrink; grids collapse to one or two columns.
< 480px
Tables reflow to cards; modals become bottom sheets; status pills become the action trigger.
Card-over-table. Below 480px a data table does not scroll horizontally — it becomes one card per row, with the two or three fields that matter and 44px actions. Hide the rest.
