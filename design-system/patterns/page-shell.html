Page Shell

Anatomy Rules

Rail & top barInjected by nav.js on every page. The rail-bottom gear is a real area, not a link — it swaps the pane to Settings.
Content offsetnav.js sets the body margin itself. A page adds no left padding of its own or the layout double-shifts.
Header rowTitle left (Lucide glyph + h1, no subtitle unless asked), controls right: store select, date navigator, then buttons — secondary before primary.
No page-level switchersSection switching lives in the nav pane only. Admin pages are reached from an .adminbtn in the tool they manage.
