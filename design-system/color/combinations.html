Unit 2.2 — Colors

Color Combinations

The book pairs colors explicitly so nothing gets over-saturated. Follow the pairs; when in doubt, more light grey and less red.

Approved Contrast Pairs

Each pair is a foreground/background combination the book signs off on. Light Red and Light Blue are the two colors allowed to lead; everything else supports them.
